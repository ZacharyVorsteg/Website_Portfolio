Who this is for

  • Owner-led and mid-sized businesses in Palm Beach County that are growing faster than their back office: contractors and home-service companies, professional services, property operators and founders running several ventures.
  • Owners who want one accountable person who builds, not a slide deck or a vendor handoff.
  • Not a fit: a large enterprise that needs a full team or a full-time AI executive, or anyone who wants a fully automated system with no person approving what sends, spends or publishes.

How to work with me

  1. Talk through one workflow. What is happening now, who uses the process, and where does it get stuck? The first conversation is free.
  2. Decide if it is worth changing. We weigh the practical value, your existing tools and the people involved. If I cannot find at least 5 hours a week to save, I will say so before we start.
  3. Agree scope in writing. Deliverables, timing, investment and responsibilities are written down before any work begins.
  4. Build, monitor and keep it running. The system is built in your accounts with a runbook, logs and alerts, and a person on your side approving what goes out.
