What changes the cost of an AI project?

  • Scope. One workflow, such as lead intake and follow-up, costs less than several systems that depend on each other.
  • Integrations. Building on the tools you already run is usually cheaper than replacing them, but each connection still has to be built and tested.
  • Control and monitoring. Approval steps for anything that sends, spends or publishes, plus logs and alerts, are part of every build and part of the price.
  • Ongoing care. A one-time handover with a runbook costs less up front than ongoing ownership, where someone keeps the system running and improves it.
