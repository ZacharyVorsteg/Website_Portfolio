What does an AI consulting engagement include, and what does it not?

An engagement covers one workflow at a time:

  • A look at how the work runs today: intake, follow-up, scheduling, reporting or documents.
  • A written scope with deliverables, timing, investment and any ongoing support, agreed before work starts.
  • The build, on the tools you already run where possible.
  • Monitoring, logs and alerts, so a failure is loud instead of silent.
  • A plain-English runbook and a handover, or ongoing care if you want it.

It does not include legal, tax, financial or compliance advice, training a model from scratch, staffing a large engineering team, or guaranteed revenue or lead numbers. If your project needs one of those, I will say so and point you to the right specialist.

What usually gets automated first?

Lead intake and routing

Every inquiry captured, checked for spam, and routed to the right person with a follow-up reminder.

Reporting in plain English

The numbers you check every morning, delivered to you, with an alert when something drifts.

Documents and proposals

Quotes, summaries and reports drafted from your data and reviewed before they go out.

How does a person stay in control of the AI?

Anything that sends, spends, publishes or deletes goes through an approval step or a hard rule written into the system. Every action is logged. Each automation can be switched off on its own without breaking the rest. This is how I run my own products, and it is the default for every build.
