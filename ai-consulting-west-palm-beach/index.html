What a first AI consulting conversation covers

We look at where hours go each week: intake, follow-up, scheduling, reporting, document work. For each one we ask three questions. Can a model do it reliably? What has to stay with a person? How will you know it is still working next month?

If there is a real system in it, I scope it in writing: deliverables, timing, investment and any ongoing support, agreed before work starts. If I can't find at least 5 hours a week to save, I'll say so before we start.
