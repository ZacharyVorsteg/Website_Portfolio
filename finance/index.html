Why FP&A automation and financial systems

Most companies do financial planning in spreadsheets — manually updating data, reconciling figures, and regenerating forecasts every month. That's expensive and error-prone. Automated FP&A systems pull data from your tools (CRM, billing, payroll) and generate live forecasts, variance reports, and dashboards. Your finance team analyzes instead of collects.

The same applies to M&A. Synergy models quantify the real value of a deal — which synergies are achievable, how long they take, and the net impact to your returns. A well-built model changes how you structure the deal and what price you should pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is FP&A automation?

FP&A automation connects financial data from tools such as billing, payroll and CRM systems to forecasts, dashboards and variance reports. Update frequency, validation checks and responsibility for reviewing the outputs are defined for the workflow.

What is M&A synergy modeling?

M&A synergy modeling estimates potential revenue, cost and working-capital effects of a transaction. The model makes assumptions, timing, implementation costs and scenarios explicit so they can be reviewed and challenged.

Can you build an investor-ready financial model?

I build three-statement models, multi-entity consolidations and pro formas for fundraising and underwriting. We agree the intended audience, required scenarios and supporting material before work starts; the model is only as useful as its assumptions and source data.
