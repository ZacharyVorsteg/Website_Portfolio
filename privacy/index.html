Who runs this site

This is the personal site of Zachary Vorsteg. Real estate references on this site relate to his work as a licensed Florida real estate Sales Associate.

Contact form

When you send a message through the contact form we receive what you type: name, email, phone, the service you select and your message, plus the page you came from and any campaign tags in the link you used (UTM or gclid). The form is processed by Netlify Forms and the submission is used only to reply to you. It is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages bring visitors. In most of the United States analytics is on by default and you can opt out at any time using the privacy choices control on each page; in the EU, EEA, UK and California it stays off until you allow it. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, Google Analytics stays off.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and reports aggregate page views without personal identifiers.

We also record first-party usage signals (page path, referring domain, campaign source, page title, viewport size, scroll depth, time on page, which buttons or links are clicked and their labels, and whether a form is viewed, started or submitted, never what is typed) on servers we operate at customlab.ai. They contain no name, email, phone number or form entries and set no cookies; a temporary per-tab identifier is discarded when the tab closes. They are not sent if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and stop from the next page you load after you choose Reject.

Cookies

Cookies on this site are limited to Google Analytics, subject to the choices above. No advertising cookies are set by this site.

Your choices and contact

To see, correct or delete information you sent, or to ask about this policy, use the contact form. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws can exercise their rights the same way.
