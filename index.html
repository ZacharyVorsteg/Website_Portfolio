01 / Commission reporting automation

Automating commission reporting for a multi-hundred-rep sales organization.

Each payout cycle meant pulling sales data, matching it to reps, applying multi-tier plan rules and building reports by hand. The approach: SQL, Python and BI reporting automation that applies plan rules, flags exceptions before release and shows attainment during the period.

Outcome: hours of manual work removed each cycle; exceptions reviewed before payment instead of corrected after.

02 / CRM & lead pipeline · My own businesses

A CRM and lead pipeline I built and run.

Trusenda is a lead-management CRM I designed and built solo for web and iOS (live on the App Store). It tracks speed to lead, deal stages and the next step on every lead. A pipeline handles scraping, enrichment and scoring, and every new lead is also handed to a separate lead desk with a 5-second timeout that can never block the save.

Outcome (small samples): found and fixed a form bug on Sept 27 that had shown visitors "Failed to submit" for three weeks. Median time to first reply went from 28 hours (Sept 15–Oct 15, 14 leads) to about 1 business hour for the 6 leads touched Oct 9–16. I keep measuring it weekly.
